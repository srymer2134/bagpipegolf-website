About the NALG Denver Invitational

Hosted by the Denver chapter of the National Association of Left-Handed Golfers (est. 1936), the Denver Invitational is a two-day, 36-hole championship at two of the region's best-conditioned munis. Field capped at 72 for tight pairings and no double-tee starts.

All scoring, live standings, pairings, and per-flight settlement is powered by Bagpipe Golf. Groups score themselves via the Bagpipe app; TDs finalize each round from the clubhouse. Spectators follow every group in real time via the share codes on the pairings sheet.

Weather & Conditions

Both days forecast partly cloudy, highs 82–86°F. Light morning wind out of the north (5–10 mph) picking up to 12–18 mph in the afternoon. Greens stimped at 11.0. Firm fairways, receptive greens.
